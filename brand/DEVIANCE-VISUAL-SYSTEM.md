# DEViance visual system — Source / Proof / Edge

## Brand idea

**The deviation is the method.**

DEViance is not a software agency and not an AI trend brand. It is Max Kle1nz's public laboratory for systems that question defaults, survive counterpoint, and expose their evidence.

## Logo system

Primary lockup: **DEV`i`ANCE.DEV**

- `DEV` + `ANCE` are set in a heavy condensed grotesk.
- The lowercase italic `i` is the only signal-colored character.
- A diagonal slash crosses the `i`: deviation exists inside the name, not beside it.
- `.DEV` is microtype, aligned to the baseline.
- The wordmark owns every public surface, including the square safe-area avatar
  and favicon treatment. The prism/trident is legacy material, not the DEViance
  signature.

Minimum clear space: the height of the capital `D`.

Never add glow, bevel, chrome, game typography, or a second signal color to the wordmark.

## Palette

- Carbon — `#050505`
- Off black — `#111111`
- Graphite — `#242424`
- Bone — `#F0EDE4`
- Concrete — `#85847F`
- Signal vermilion — `#FF4A1F` (hero and decisive declarations)
- Source blue — `#2349D8` (provenance, focus, captured evidence)
- Action oxide — `#B83817` (destinations, action and handoff)

Ratio: 70% carbon or paper / 20% atmosphere / 5% source / 5% action.

Colors have roles, not moods: blue always means where the claim came from;
oxide always means where the reader can act.

## Typography

- Display: Archivo Narrow Bold or a severe condensed grotesk.
- Reading: Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Technical: IBM Plex Mono Medium.
- Macro type is architecture.
- Micro type is evidence.
- Use uppercase for declarations, sentence case for explanations.

## Composition

One monumental statement. Technical margins. Severe crops. Intentional
negative space. One impossible gesture per scene.

The grid must remain visible enough to prove structure. Texture may age the
surface but never hide hierarchy.

Three proprietary gestures govern evidence surfaces:

- **Source Seam:** a two-pixel blue line physically binding source metadata to
  every repository.
- **Continuation Cut:** every repository plate loses a 48×48 corner and the
  real destination crosses that cut.
- **Proof Ruler 01–05:** five pressure marks; the fifth breaks the text boundary
  to make handoff structural.

## Image direction

Obsidian bodies, sealed optics, smoked glass, leather, chrome, xerox, film grain, and one signal line. Human authority meets machine ambiguity.

Avoid neon cities, generic cyberpunk, blue-magenta gradients, decorative glitch, floating dashboards, glassmorphism, and videogame key art.

## Motion

Motion is consequence, not atmosphere.

- No entry choreography, scanline, ticker, blinking status, counter or parallax.
- Hover and active states move no more than two pixels.
- Links go directly to their destination; no confirmation ritual.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete composition without displacement.

## Voice

First person. Concrete. Evidence first. No startup adjectives.

Preferred verbs: build, seal, break, prove, ship, hand off, survive.

Preferred phrases:

- The deviation is the method.
- Green without proof is noise.
- The repos are the pitch.
- I build the first impossible version. You make it survive.
- Intelligence beyond the edge.
